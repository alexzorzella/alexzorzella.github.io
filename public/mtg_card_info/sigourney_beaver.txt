Sigourney Beaver is an unreleased card from Magic the Gathering: Edge of Eternities. The card was scrapped early in research and development, but was leaked among several tax documents and restaurant orders from a subpeona of the Royal High District Court of New London. The original artwork for the card is still searched for to this day.
Building a commander deck with your Sigourney Beaver commander is simple: keywords are the key. Focusing on a couple of the keywords is essential.
